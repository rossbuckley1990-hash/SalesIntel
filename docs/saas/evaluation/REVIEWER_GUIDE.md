# Opportunity review: a guide for reviewers

Thank you for helping. You will judge 44 possible sales opportunities, one at a time, as a fire and security salesperson would.

- It takes about 90 minutes in total.
- You can stop and come back as often as you like.

## What you receive

One file, named like `opportunity-review-opportunities-2026-11-05-v2-reviewer-a.html`. It is yours alone. Do not forward it.

## How to do it

1. **Open the file.** Save it to your computer and open it in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. It works offline and sends nothing anywhere.
2. **Start.** Enter your name, and confirm two things:
   - you will work alone, and will not discuss any item with anyone until the review is closed;
   - you will judge each item only on the evidence shown, and will not look for how the items were found.
3. **Judge each item.** Read its evidence, and open the source links if you wish. Then answer the nine questions. Your answers are saved in this browser as you go.
4. **Send your answers.** When you have judged every item, click **Download my answers** and email the file to the person running the review. Keep a copy until they confirm it arrived.

## Things to know

- **How items were found.** Some were found by SalesIntel and some by other methods. You are not told which, and you should not try to find out.
- **No right answers.** We want your honest view as a salesperson. Judge each item on its own.
- **Changing computer.** Download your answers on the first computer. On the second, use **Load your answers file** on the start screen.
- **Browser data.** If you clear it before downloading, your answers are lost. Download your answers at the end of each sitting.
- **Afterwards.** If a disagreement between reviewers is discussed later, the person running the review will arrange it. Until then, please keep your answers to yourself.

## The nine questions

| Question | What it asks |
|---|---|
| Is there a genuine fire or security commercial opportunity here for a firm like yours? | Your overall view: yes, possibly or no. |
| Is the organisation (and site, if named) correctly identified from the evidence? | Whether the evidence really is about the organisation shown. |
| Is a customer requirement confirmed, or only hypothesised? | Confirmed means the evidence states a need, such as a tender. Hypothesised means it may need something. |
| Has it already been advertised? | An open tender or published notice that competitors can also see. |
| Is there a legitimate reason to contact them now? | Something you could honestly open a conversation with. |
| Can you tell who to speak to? | A named person, a role only, or no. |
| Is a reliable business contact route available? | A route you would trust, such as an official portal or a published business contact. |
| How strong and current is the evidence? | Strong, adequate or weak. |
| Would you pursue it? | This week, this month, later, or no. |
